- The next steps `atcr review` and `atcr status` print (`Next:` lines, or `help[]` under `--axi`) now follow what the review holds: a review with no reconciled data yet suggests `atcr reconcile <id>` instead of `atcr report <id>` and `atcr verify <id>`, which exit 2 until it runs; a failed review suggests `atcr status <id>` and `atcr review --resume <id>`; a still-running one no longer suggests `report`. A review that reconciled in-process (`--fail-on` or `fail_on` in config, `--verify`, `--debate`, `--auto-fix`) still suggests `report` and `verify`. See [docs/status.md](docs/status.md). (epic 114)
