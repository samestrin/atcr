- `atcr debt validate --coherence` scores each row's Fix against its Problem by the cosine of their embeddings from the `context_prefetch.semantic` embedding endpoint (no qdrant needed) and flags the least coherent `--coherence-percentile` percent (default 20). Every Problem and Fix passes the `secret_guard` scan before it is sent; with no endpoint configured, a text the guard blocks, or an endpoint that fails at any point, the coherence result is `skipped` with a class-only reason and nothing partial. Flagged rows are advisory (exit 0). (epic 96)
