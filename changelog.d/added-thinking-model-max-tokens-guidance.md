- `docs/registry.md` now recommends declaring `max_tokens: 16384` on thinking-model agents, and explains why the built-in output cap for an agent with no `max_tokens` stays at 8192: at that cap thinking models can run out of output mid-reasoning (`finish_reason=length`) and return no findings, but a 16384 default would leave every tool-loop agent on a window of 53248 tokens or less, including the 32768 default, with no input budget. (EST-5119)
