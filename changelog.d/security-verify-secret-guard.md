- `atcr verify` now applies `secret_guard` to what it sends a model: the skeptic prompt, the agent-mode fixer's tool loop and the single-shot fixer prompt are scanned under the registry's policy (`warn` when unset). `warn` logs and sends, `redact` replaces the credential, and `block` sends nothing: the skeptic records the finding `unverifiable` and the fixer records a `secret_guard=block` fix warning. The policy is read from the registry tier only; a project-tier `secret_guard` does not reach verify yet. (epic 106.3)
